# S-06.02.42 — Safe human-readable enrichment exports (audit A19 / D-272)

**Status: IN_REVIEW, no repository-suite or live spreadsheet proof.** Role: security engineer. Branch: develop.

## Risk
Apollo/imported contact fields become CSV/XLSX cells, where a string beginning with =, +, -, @ (possibly after whitespace/control characters) can be evaluated as a spreadsheet formula. CSV quoting is not a defence. The original records and internal deduplication keys must not be rewritten merely to protect a human export.

## Acceptance
- All human-readable reports from `io_utils.write_outputs`, snapshot/analytics exports and existing-POI manifest/raw-input exports escape dangerous string cells on output (prefix a literal apostrophe).
- XLSX generated by pandas/openpyxl contains strings, not formula-typed cells, for those inputs; numbers stay numeric.
- Saved CRM rows, JSONL responses and authoritative internal credit/exclusion/rejection ledgers keep their exact original values. Their *human-facing copies* are escaped.
- The protection covers accepted and rejected rows, untrusted names, notes, URLs, error text and summaries. No formula is executed while exporting.

## Implementation
`io_utils.spreadsheet_safe_frame` copies and guards export values, leaving source frames untouched. `existing_poi_enrichment.py` guards report-only CSVs. `attempt_ledger.py` guards the report snapshot, not the persistent credit ledger. `tests/test_spreadsheet_exports.py` checks CSV and workbook cell types as well as numeric preservation.

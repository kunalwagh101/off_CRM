## What the regression tests protect

- Apply registers a disabled local posting/image pair and its own rate. Start
  requires the install owner, current plan-line links, valid campaigns and
  owned enabled accounts. Historical plan versions keep their own metadata.
- Explicit Start after a global stop does not activate other old pairs. Paused,
  disabled and zero-rate pairs retain queued posts until explicitly resumed.
- Approval, skip and scheduling are workspace scoped. Concurrent next-slot
  allocation is transactional. Legacy offsets compare actual UTC instants.
- A malformed legacy date is quarantined with a safe reference; valid posts
  continue, next-slot allocation succeeds, and publish results expose the
  failure to automation status. Published receipts are preserved.
- Pacing proposals are per pair and bind campaign, identity and original rate.
  Accept/dismiss cannot apply a stale/other pair's proposal. Account caps follow
  the pair's selected accounts. Configuration writers use root/config leases.
- Missing, foreign or disappeared attachments fail closed at approval,
  scheduling, publishing and the outbox adapter. Explicit assetless posts work.
  Valid video export still publishes; failed export cannot publish its caption.
- UI attachment fetching is authenticated, lazy (one active preview), image or
  video typed, abortable and capped at 32 MiB. Server-declared missing/foreign
  media cannot receive the reviewed-elsewhere acknowledgement override.

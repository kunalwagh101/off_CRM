# Work order — S-06.02.25 every screen opens at its top, its warnings in view

## ID
S-06.02.25 — Every screen opens at its top, its warnings in view.
Board: READY. Fixes `D-137`. Size S. Frontend only.

## GOAL
When the owner opens a screen, its title and first panel are in view below the
top bar — and when the campaign list is failing, the "Could not refresh
campaigns" panel is in view too, because it says why campaign work is blocked.
Keyboard and screen-reader focus still moves to the main content.

## BLAST RADIUS / CONSUMERS
- `frontend/src/App.tsx` — `focusMainContent()` and the `useEffect` that calls
  it on every page change; `<main id="main-content" tabIndex={-1}>`.
- `frontend/src/styles.css` — `.topbar` is `position: sticky; top: 0` and 68px
  high (60px under the phone breakpoint).
- Every screen: this is layout, not a screen's own code.
- `S-06.02.05` (accessibility, IN_REVIEW) owns the focus contract: after a
  route change, focus lands on the main content. It must keep holding.

## WHAT WAS SEEN
Real Chromium, 1280×900, `#settings` opened while `/api/v1/campaigns` answered
500: the Settings title sat half under the top bar and the error panel was
above the viewport. Scrolled to the top, both were there. TEST 1 below is the
check that showed it, written out.

## ROOT CAUSE — confirm before fixing
Two suspects; step 1 of the TEST decides which:
1. `focusMainContent()` calls `element.focus()` without `{ preventScroll: true }`.
   The browser scrolls the main area's top to the top of the viewport, which is
   under the 68px sticky bar.
2. The error panel renders after that scroll, above the focused content, and
   the browser's scroll anchoring keeps the content where it was — so the panel
   stays out of view.

## DESIGN
- Focus without scrolling, then put the page at its top:
  `main.focus({ preventScroll: true }); window.scrollTo({ top: 0 });` on a page
  change. Opening a screen means seeing its top; there is no saved position to
  keep.
- Give `#main-content` `scroll-margin-top` equal to the top bar's height, so any
  later `scrollIntoView` also clears the bar.
- If suspect 2 is confirmed, render the campaign error panel inside the same
  frame as the screen, or scroll to the top when the error first appears, not
  on every refresh.

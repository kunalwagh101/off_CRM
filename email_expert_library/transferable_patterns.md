# OffsetX email expert patterns

These are product-owned working notes. They describe transferable principles, not any named writer's distinctive voice.

## First touch

- Open with one verified public fact that is relevant to the recipient.
- Connect that fact to one operational tension.
- Explain OffsetX in one plain sentence.
- Ask one route-appropriate question.
- Future-client outreach earns interest before asking for time.
- Expert outreach may ask for a 15-minute pressure test.

## Follow-ups

- Add one useful angle instead of repeating the first email.
- Keep the original thread and subject.
- Stop the sequence as soon as a reply is detected.
- The final note closes the loop without guilt or artificial urgency.

## Safety

- Never invent a hook, claim, relationship, quote, result or source.
- Never expose internal architecture, customer data, pricing or lead-source details.
- Human approval is required before a message becomes eligible to send.
- Store source provenance and a rights basis for every imported expert document.
